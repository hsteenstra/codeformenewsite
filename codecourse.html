Meet CodeCourse.

A digital learning experience designed to make learning programming more interactive, approachable, and fun.

IN DEVELOPMENT
THE IDEA

Learn coding one step at a time.

CodeCourse is being built as a digital learning platform for students who want to learn programming at their own pace.

We're designing it around short lessons, practice, projects, and immediate feedback.

01

Short lessons

Break programming concepts into manageable pieces instead of overwhelming beginners.

02

Practice

Give students opportunities to actually use the concepts they're learning.

03

Projects

Move beyond exercises and give students opportunities to build something of their own.
