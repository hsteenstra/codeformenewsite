WHY CODE FOR ME?

Technology should be a tool, not a barrier.

Code For ME is a student-led organization creating free and affordable opportunities for Maine students to explore computer science.

We believe students shouldn't need expensive programs, advanced equipment, or connections in the technology industry to start creating.
